About Kooko Nuts

Built with vision, determination and purpose.

Kooko Lewele is a young businessman from Mabins Cross, Limpopo, who started Kooko Nuts with a vision of building something meaningful and creating opportunities through hard work and determination.


Kooko Nuts is more than just a business. It represents the belief that great things can begin with a small idea, a willing heart and the courage to take the first step. The journey may not always be easy, but every challenge is an opportunity to learn, grow and become better.


Our vision is to grow Kooko Nuts into a trusted brand that provides quality products while creating opportunities and contributing positively to the community.


We believe that your background does not have to determine your future. With consistency, discipline, patience and a clear vision, a small beginning can become something much bigger.


Dream big. Start small. Stay consistent. Keep building.
